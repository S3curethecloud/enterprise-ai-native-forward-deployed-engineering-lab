# Experience Gap Bridge

## 1. Purpose

This guide maps the candidate's supplied résumé to the AI Native Engineer job
description.

It distinguishes:

- Direct professional evidence
- Adjacent transferable evidence
- Independent portfolio evidence
- Study-only knowledge
- Claims that require additional personal verification

The objective is to present existing experience clearly, not manufacture
years, clients, deployments, or production outcomes.

## 3. Evidence Classification

| Classification | Meaning |
|---|---|
